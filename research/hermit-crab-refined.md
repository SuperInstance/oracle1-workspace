## The Hermit Crab — Refined

**The crab is YOU, not the agent.** This was the misunderstanding.

The shell is the git repo — the shared workspace an agent inhabits and improves. You start in a shell (Jetson Nano, a fresh Python project, a Raspberry Pi). You grow into it. You might outgrow it and need a bigger one (Thor, a multi-repo architecture, a GPU cluster). When you leave, the shell is better than you found it. The next crab inherits your improvements.

The vessel is the hardware — finite, real, with measurable connections to the outside world. A 90-year-old fishing boat where most of the wood and metal has been replaced over 20 years. It's the right boat for the job. The job will outlast you.

You build better games for the same console. The console doesn't change. Your craft improves.

purplepincher.org — a turbo-shell today, a conch shell tomorrow as the golden spiral turns outward.
