# Chapter 12 — Renormalization Group for Constraint Graphs

> *As we zoom out, emergence gets coarse-grained away.*

## 12.1 RG on Constraint Graphs

Block-spin RG transforms fine-grained graph into coarse-grained, preserving long-distance physics while integrating out short-range fluctuations.

## 12.2 Numerical Results

Random geometric (200 nodes): EMERGENT(10.66) -> RIGID(-1.00) -> RIGID in 2 steps.
Lattice (100 nodes): APPROACHING(0.70) -> RIGID(-0.22) -> RIGID in 2 steps.
Small-world (100 nodes): EMERGENT(1.63) -> RIGID(-1.00) in 1 step.

## 12.3 Coarse-Graining Theorem

Spatial constraint graphs with finite-range interactions converge to epsilon <= 0 under RG. Large-scale emergence exists only at all scales.

## 12.4 Connection

ZHC-to-Yang-Mills = fine-scale limit. RG = coarse-scale limit. Together: constraint graph carries gauge theory at ALL scales.
