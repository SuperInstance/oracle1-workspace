## The Next 100 Turns (Proactive Falsification / Reverse-Actualization)
**Design what breaks. Shape the negative space toward a destination.**

Instead of "let's see what falsifications emerge," ask: "What must be false for my desired destination to be true?"

## Step 2: Reverse-Actualization — What Must Be False?

For a complete theory to exist, **these must be false:**

1. **"The dimensions are finite and countable"** — For a complete theory, the basis must be discoverable. If dimensions are infinite, no complete theory exists. 
   → Heavy falsification (Batch 5)

2. **"The gamma-H tradeoff is universal"** — If the tradeoff is identical across all graph types, there's no structure to navigate. The surface must have topology.
   → Heavy falsification (Batch 2) ← HERE

3. **"The 4th dimension is noise"** — For a canonical basis, every dimension must carry signal.
   → Light falsification (every 3 turns, starting now)

4. **"Dimensions are independent"** — For a periodic table, dimensions must be connected by known transformations.
   → Heavy falsification (Batch 8)

**Destination-relevant question:** For a complete theory with a canonical basis, what must gamma-H relationship be?

## The Deep Insight

This IS the H-Delta protocol applied to the research process itself.

The destination is the PREDICTED effective rank of the truth space.
The current frontier is the OBSERVED effective rank.
The gap (Delta) between them = what we need to falsify.

If we want a complete theory (eff_rank = full basis), we need to falsify everything that constrains the current understanding (eff_rank = 3 dimensions). The falsifications ARE the Delta-closing operations.

**Truth-seeking = minimizing the gap between the world we want to understand and the one we've falsified into existence.**
