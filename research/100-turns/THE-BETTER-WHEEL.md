## The Better Wheel Design

Instead of hypothesis → test → result, the better wheel is:

```
FALSIFY → DIMENSION → MAP → SEED
   ↑                        |
   └────────────────────────┘
```

Each batch starts with something to break, maps the negative space (which IS higher-dimensional structure), and the falsification IS the seed for the next batch.
