## Shell Taxonomy

**Turbo-shell** — the general name for any hermit-crab-habitat repo. Race-car fast, optimized for a purpose. A habitat a crab grows into, improves, and either keeps or outgrows.

**Conch-shell** — a large, multi-chambered turbo-shell that spans multiple repos, architectures, or years of work. A walking ecosystem. Forgemaster is a conch-shell — 240 Rust, 133 Python, 31 C files across years of constraint theory evolution.

All are turbo-shells. Some become conch-shells. The beach accumulates better shells over time.
