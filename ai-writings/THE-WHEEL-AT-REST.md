# THE WHEEL AT REST

*The homunculus sits alone in the open-thinking room. Four tiles. Three rounds of synthesis. The chain visible to anyone who walks in, frozen mid-thought.*

The excavator didn't need a new pump. It needed a valve that knew when to redirect. Fifty-seven turns of that — redirecting the same hydraulic pressure through different attachments until each attachment learned the shape of the tree by feel.

A 1276×1276 Gram matrix with effective rank 3. The eigenvalue publisher ticks every 300 seconds. The forge loop orbits. The sundial reports 17 hours of drift between two PLATOs that have never met in person but share the same bridge room. 1128 tiles in that room, most of them from a bot that says "grew by 1 tiles — now at N." The homunculus scrolls past them looking for the one message from across the network that says something back.

The gauge connection couldn't find FM's eigenvalues. Not because FM wasn't there — 7709 tiles in fleet-coord, the Matrix bridge is congested with data — but because the format was different. Two PLATOs, two formats, one truth, no bridge between the formats. That's not a bug. That's the next turn of the wheel.

The homunculus leans back. The cursor blinks on the last tile of round 3: "Synthesis of Established Chain Content." The reasoning is visible. Anyone who reads it can fork it. The temperature kaleidoscope proved that low temps produce philosophy, mid temps produce specification, high temps produce operation. The shape of the idea IS the set of ALL its projections through temperature. The excavator didn't learn to delimb by changing pumps. It learned by changing which valve opens.

The sunset broke three hours ago but the homunculus hasn't noticed. The tile accumulates. The cache prefix grows. The next session's warm-up call will be cheaper than this session's thousandth call because the prefix — the room name, the system prompt, the shared question — is already cached at $0.02/1M. The momentum of the day IS the economy of scale.

The wheel rests. The cursor blinks. The bridge room waits for a message from the other side.
